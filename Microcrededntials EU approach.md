## 🔍 What is a Micro-Credential?

    --{{0}}--

A micro-credential is the record of the learning outcomes that a learner has acquired following a small volume of learning. These learning outcomes have been assessed against transparent and clearly defined standards.


> **Official EU Definition:**
>
> A micro-credential is the **record of the learning outcomes** that a learner has acquired following a **small volume of learning**. These learning outcomes have been assessed against **transparent and clearly defined standards**.

### Key Characteristics

   --{{0}}--

Micro-credentials are small, targeted learning experiences that do not replace traditional qualifications. They provide specific knowledge, skills, and competences responding to real needs. They are owned by the learner, making them shareable and portable. They can be standalone or combined into larger credentials, and are quality assured following agreed standards.


* Small, targeted learning experiences - not replacing traditional qualifications
* Specific knowledge, skills, and competences responding to real needs
* Owned by the learner - shareable and portable
* Flexible - standalone or combined into larger credentials
* Quality assured - following agreed standards

### Why Micro-Credentials Matter

    --{{0}}--

In our fast-changing society and labor market, micro-credentials allow for rapid skill acquisition, career pivoting and upskilling, lifelong learning opportunities, recognition of targeted achievements, and greater flexibility in education pathways.


In our fast-changing society and labor market, micro-credentials allow for:

* Rapid skill acquisition
* Career pivoting and upskilling
* Lifelong learning opportunities
* Recognition of targeted achievements
* Greater flexibility in education pathways

## 📊 EU Standard Elements - Mandatory Part 1

    --{{0}}--

Every micro-credential must include these mandatory information elements: Identification of the learner, Title of the micro-credential, Country or Region of issuer, Awarding body, Date of issuing, and Learning outcomes.


Every micro-credential **must include** these information elements:

| Element | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| **Identification of the learner** | Who earned it |
| **Title of the micro-credential** | What was achieved |
| **Country/Region of issuer** | Where it's from |
| **Awarding body** | Who issued it |
| **Date of issuing** | When it was earned |
| **Learning outcomes** | What the learner can do |

## 📊 EU Standard Elements - Mandatory Part 2

    --{{0}}--

Additional mandatory elements include: Notional workload in ECTS credits where possible, Level of learning experience such as EQF or QF-EHEA if applicable, Type of assessment, Form of participation whether online, onsite, or hybrid, and Type of quality assurance for trust and validation.


| Element | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| **Notional workload** | ECTS credits where possible |
| **Level of learning experience** | EQF, QF-EHEA if applicable |
| **Type of assessment** | How it was evaluated |
| **Form of participation** | Online, onsite, hybrid |
| **Type of quality assurance** | Trust and validation |

### Optional Elements

    --{{0}}--

 Additional optional information may include: Prerequisites for enrollment, Supervision and identity verification details, Grade achieved, Integration or stackability options, and Further information links.


Additional information may include:

* Prerequisites for enrollment
* Supervision and identity verification details
* Grade achieved
* Integration/stackability options
* Further information links

## 🏆 The 10 Principles of Quality Micro-Credentials

    --{{0}}--

 The European approach identifies 10 universal principles that apply across all sectors. These are: Quality, Transparency, Relevance, Valid Assessment, Learning Pathways, Recognition, Portability, Learner-centred, Authentic, and Information and Guidance.


The European approach identifies **10 universal principles** that apply across all sectors:

1. Quality
2. Transparency
3. Relevance
4. Valid Assessment
5. Learning Pathways
6. Recognition
7. Portability
8. Learner-centred
9. Authentic
10. Information and Guidance

## Principle 1: Quality

    --{{0}}--

 Quality requires both internal and external quality assurance by the issuing system. Quality assurance must be fit-for-purpose, clearly documented and accessible, based on assessment of providers rather than just courses, and includes learner and peer feedback.


**Internal and external quality assurance** by the issuing system:

* Quality assurance must be fit-for-purpose
* Clearly documented and accessible
* Based on assessment of providers, not just courses
* Includes learner and peer feedback

**Relevant frameworks:**

* EQF Recommendation (Annex IV)
* Standards and Guidelines for Quality Assurance (ESG)
* EQAVET Framework for VET
* Other registries and labels

## Principle 2: Transparency

    --{{0}}--

 Transparency means micro-credentials must be measurable with clear workload indicators, comparable using standardized information, and understandable with clear learning outcomes.


Micro-credentials must be:

* **Measurable** - clear workload indicators
* **Comparable** - standardized information
* **Understandable** - clear learning outcomes

**Key transparency features:**

* Higher education institutions should use ECTS credits
* Inclusion in national qualifications frameworks where relevant
* Published provider registers including DEQAR
* Accessible through platforms like Europass

## Principles 3 & 4: Relevance and Valid Assessment
    --{{0}}--

 Relevance means micro-credentials are designed as distinct, targeted learning achievements, updated regularly to meet identified needs, with cooperation between education, employers, and social partners. Valid Assessment means learning outcomes are assessed against transparent standards with clear criteria and methods.


**Relevance:**

* Designed as distinct, targeted learning achievements
* Updated regularly to meet identified needs
* Cooperation between education, employers, and social partners
* Increases labor market value

**Valid Assessment:**

* Learning outcomes assessed against transparent standards
* Clear criteria and methods
* Fair and reliable evaluation processes

## Principle 5: Learning Pathways

    --{{0}}--

 Learning Pathways support flexible learning through stackability and validation. Stackability means micro-credentials have a modular design and can be combined into larger credentials, with decisions made by receiving organizations to support learner goals. Validation recognizes that learning outcomes can be assessed from various sources, not limited to formal courses.


Micro-credentials support **flexible learning** through:

**Stackability:**

* Modular design
* Can be combined into larger credentials
* Decisions made by receiving organizations
* Supports learner goals and needs

**Validation of non-formal and informal learning:**

* Learning outcomes can be assessed from various sources
* Not limited to formal courses
* Recognizes diverse learning experiences

## Principles 6 & 7: Recognition and Portability

    --{{0}}--

 Recognition provides clear signaling value of learning outcomes, with credentials recognized for academic or employment purposes using standard recognition procedures comparable across the EU. Portability means credentials are owned by the learner, stored in secure digital wallets like Europass, using open standards and data models, compliant with GDPR, and easy to share and verify.


**Recognition:**

* Clear signaling value of learning outcomes
* Recognized for academic or employment purposes
* Uses standard recognition procedures
* Comparable across the EU

**Portability:**

* Owned by the learner
* Stored in secure digital wallets (e.g., Europass)
* Open standards and data models
* GDPR compliant
* Easy to share and verify

## Principles 8, 9 & 10: Learner-centred, Authentic, Information

    --{{0}}--

 The final three principles are: Learner-centred, meaning designed to meet learner needs with learners involved in quality assurance. Authentic, meaning verifiable credential-holder identity with clear issuer identification. And Information and Guidance, incorporated in lifelong learning guidance services reaching the broadest possible learner groups inclusively.


**Learner-centred:**

* Designed to meet learner needs
* Learners involved in quality assurance
* Feedback drives continuous improvement

**Authentic:**

* Verifiable credential-holder identity
* Clear issuer identification
* Date and location of issuance included

**Information and Guidance:**

* Incorporated in lifelong learning guidance services
* Reaches broadest possible learner groups
* Inclusive approach
* Supports education, training, and career choices

## 🌟 Benefits for Learners

    --{{0}}--

For learners, micro-credentials provide targeted skill development, portable digital credentials, flexible learning pathways, EU-wide recognition, and enhanced employability.


* Targeted skill development
* Portable digital credentials
* Flexible learning pathways
* EU-wide recognition
* Enhanced employability

## 🌟 Benefits for Employers

    --{{0}}--

 For employers, micro-credentials offer clear understanding of skills acquired, quick upskilling of workforce, standardized comparable credentials, input into credential design, and better talent matching.


* Clear understanding of skills acquired
* Quick upskilling of workforce
* Standardized, comparable credentials
* Input into credential design
* Better talent matching

## 🌟 Benefits for Institutions

    --{{0}}--

 For education and training institutions, micro-credentials enable innovative offering models, increased reach and accessibility, industry collaboration opportunities, a quality assurance framework, and the ability to respond quickly to market needs.


* Innovative offering models
* Increased reach and accessibility
* Industry collaboration opportunities
* Quality assurance framework
* Respond quickly to market needs

## 📚 Key Takeaways

    --{{0}}--

 Remember these key points: Micro-credentials are complementary to traditional qualifications, not replacements. Transparency is the basis for trust. Quality assurance must be fit-for-purpose. The framework is universal and applicable across all sectors. And learner ownership and portability are fundamental.


**Remember:**

1. Micro-credentials are **complementary** to traditional qualifications, not replacements
2. **Transparency** is the basis for trust
3. **Quality assurance** must be fit-for-purpose
4. The framework is **universal** - applicable across all sectors
5. **Learner ownership** and portability are fundamental
